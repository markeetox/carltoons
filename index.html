How to Play

The Egg

When you first join, you get an egg. Every day for 3 days you must perform one action to influence your future pigeon's stats:

🫳
Shake — boosts YOLO & FOMO (aggressive, fast)
🔥
Heat — boosts HODL & WAGMI (tanky, resilient)
👅
Lick — boosts FUD & NGMI (chaotic, big HP)

After 3 days your egg hatches into a unique pigeon — one of 3,888 possible combinations.

Daily Care

Once hatched, your pigeon needs daily attention. You get one of each action per day — they reset at midnight.

Feed — increases bond +8
Play — increases bond +10
Train — increases bond +5, grows your weakest stat

Stats

YOLOAttack power — high risk, high reward
FOMOSpeed — who moves first in battle
HODLDefense — reduces damage taken
FUDDebuff — chance to confuse opponent
NGMIHP pool — how much punishment you can take
WAGMIRecovery — HP regen between rounds

Tooniseum Battles

Challenge other players in turn-based battles. Each round both players pick a move simultaneously — Rock Paper Scissors style:

🐦 Peckbeats💨 Dodge
🌀 Swoopbeats🐦 Peck
💨 Dodgebeats🌀 Swoop

Your stats modify the outcome — YOLO adds damage variance, FUD can make opponents miss, FOMO determines round order. Win to gain ELO, lose to drop it.

Streaks & Penalties

Miss 1 dayBond −10. Your pigeon is cold.
Miss 2–3 daysBond −20, one random stat −2, battle debuff next fight.
Miss 4+ daysFERAL MODE. Bond & stats decay daily. Pigeon vibrates with rage.

💡 Returning from feral? Log in 3 days in a row to earn your pigeon's trust back.
